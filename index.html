A2Z / Who they are

A training and creative workshop academy in Gaza

Who are they?
A place to explore your potential.

A2Z offers courses and workshops combining digital skills and artistic experiences, connecting learning with practice.

From editing a video or creating an AI image to shaping a piece of clay by hand.

Every step adds to your abilities

02

03
